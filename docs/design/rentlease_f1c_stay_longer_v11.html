Staying longer · v11

Cut-down "Ask to extend": +1, +3 and +6 months or a custom date, the first clashing date in the error, no counter-offer and no notifications.

What changed from v10

  • Counter-offer removed. The owner can only Accept or Decline, with an optional note on Decline.
  • Custom date picker added. Dates run from the day after the current end to 6 months later. The rest are greyed.
  • A clash shows the first blocked date, for example "Another stay starts Mar 10", with no names. It appears when the tenant picks a date and again if the owner taps Accept after something changed.
  • No push notification. The owner finds the request under "Needs your attention" on the Tenancy tab and in Manage "Needs you".
  • A pending request disappears from the screen when the tenancy is no longer active.

What the server decides

  • Only approved, unexpired reservations from other people, and other proposed, pending or active tenancies on the same property, block an extension. A pending reservation does not.
  • One open request per tenancy. Accept moves only the end date. Rent and due day do not change.
  • Errors the app maps by message: not allowed, unavailable (reason), date invalid (why), conflict with date, and changed so reload.
