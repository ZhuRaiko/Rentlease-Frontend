  • ②Upload states live on the thumbnails. Uploading shows a veil on that thumbnail, a failed upload shows an amber veil with Retry. Reuse the existing photo workflow (load, queue, resume, reconcile); only the surface changes. Continue waits for uploads and says why, as in Stage 2.
