Reviews belong to the property · Themes first · relist keeps them

Proposal for you to approve. Same look as v8. Three changes: (1) a review is about the property, never titled with the owner; (2) the ratings section opens on Themes when there is something to show, otherwise on Reviews; (3) listing a property again keeps its reviews. Nothing here is built yet.

What this changes

  • Property, not post. A review is attached to the property record. Relisting, pausing or archiving never resets it. Think of rating a restaurant, not a menu post.
  • Owner rating stays, but derived. It is the average of reviews across the owner's properties and shows only on the owner row and profile. No separate owner form.
  • Themes first. Tag counts drawn as a sized word cloud. No external service needed for this view, because tags come from fixed lists.
  • Rule for the default tab: Themes when the property has at least 3 tag selections, otherwise Reviews only (no tabs), and a quiet empty card when there are none.
  • Backend: none. The audit confirmed reviews are stored under the property id and relisting reuses the same property, so nothing is lost today. The gap is wording: "Create a new listing" feels like starting over.

5 · List again (landlord)

  • On an archived or ended property, the single filled button is "List again". The sheet states the promise: reviews and rating stay.
  • Starts the compose flow for the same property (photos and details are on the property, so they carry over). To check on device: how much of the form is prefilled.
  • While a property has no live listing, the public cannot see its reviews. They come back when it is listed again. Existing Listed / Paused / Archived rules apply. Pause and archive stay disabled while a tenancy is active.
